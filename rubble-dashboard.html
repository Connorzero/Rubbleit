How quoting works
When a customer posts a job near you, you'll see the job type, location and size. Up to 3 businesses can quote on each job.

If the customer picks your quote, their details are revealed and you pay a 12% success fee (min £5, max £60). Quotes you lose cost nothing. The success fee is charged when a customer accepts your quote and is non-refundable.
